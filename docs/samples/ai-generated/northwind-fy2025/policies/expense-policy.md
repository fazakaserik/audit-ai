# Employee Expense Policy — Northwind Traders

Version 2025.1

## Purpose

This policy sets rules for reimbursable business expenses.

## Meal limits

- Domestic travel meals: maximum **USD 75 per person per day**.
- International travel meals: maximum USD 120 per person per day.
- Alcohol is not reimbursable unless pre-approved for a client entertainment event.

## Receipts

Original itemised receipts are required for any expense of **USD 25 or more**. Soft copies uploaded to the expense system are acceptable.

## Approval

Managers must approve expense reports within ten business days. Reports above USD 2,000 require director approval.
