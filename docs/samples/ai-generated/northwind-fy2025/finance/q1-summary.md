## 1. Executive overview

Q1 FY2025 total revenue was **USD 5,030,000**, up **8%** versus Q1 FY2024 (USD 4,657,000). Product-only gross margin was **40.9%** (up 40 bps vs Q1 FY2024) on favourable mix in the Pacific Northwest region; blended gross margin on total revenue was **45.0%**. Operating expenses were contained relative to plan; EBITDA margin was 18.4%.

Cash and cash equivalents at 31 March 2025: **USD 2,150,000**.
