# Master Services Agreement — Contoso Ltd and Northwind Traders

Effective date: 1 January 2025.

## 1. Parties

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is between Contoso Ltd ("Supplier") and Northwind Traders ("Customer").

## 2. Payment terms

### 2.1 Invoices

Supplier shall invoice Customer monthly in arrears. Payment is due within **30 days** of the invoice date ("Net-30").

### 2.2 Late payment

Overdue amounts accrue interest at 1.5% per month, capped at the maximum rate permitted by law.

## 3. Term and termination

The initial term is twelve (12) months from the effective date. Either party may terminate for convenience with sixty (60) days written notice.

## 4. Liability

Except for wilful misconduct, each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid in the twelve months preceding the claim.
